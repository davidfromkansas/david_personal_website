About me

As a Product Manager, David brings expertise in AI-driven personalization for shopping from eBay and experience in social commerce and growth from working on consumer product at Twitter.

He has a successful track record of getting 0-to-1 products off the ground by listening to customer needs and forming data-driven hypotheses that unlock Product Market Fit.

Most of all, David enjoys rallying xfn teams of passionate engineers, designers, and researchers around our customers, which have included everyday people, businesses, and advertisers.

David has a B.S. in Computer Science from Washington University in Saint Louis, where he was classically trained as a software engineer.
